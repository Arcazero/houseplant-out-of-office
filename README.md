One out-of-office auto-reply, at most 120 characters, written as if a
houseplant had sent it. `main.py` writes it to `/output/entry.txt`,
where the make.fun judge compares it two at a time against the other
entries.

> Out of office: the fern hasn't had water since March, so it's returning the favour — your email will be read in spring.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
